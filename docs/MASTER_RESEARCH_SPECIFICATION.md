# MASTER_RESEARCH_SPECIFICATION
## Part 1 — Vision, Research Philosophy, Scientific Method

**Version:** 0.1 Draft

---

# 1. Purpose

This project exists to determine whether a discretionary market-structure methodology can be transformed into an objective, statistically validated, reproducible trading framework for XAUUSD.

The objective is **not** to prove or defend ICT, SMC, Smart Money Concepts, or any discretionary methodology.

The objective is to evaluate every hypothesis under controlled empirical conditions and retain only those supported by evidence.

No concept receives privileged treatment.

Every concept must earn its place through research.

---

# 2. Final Objective

The final deliverable is a research-backed execution engine capable of:

- determining market context,
- identifying objectively defined structural opportunities,
- filtering low-quality environments,
- executing only statistically supported setups,
- adapting risk to market conditions,
- remaining reproducible across different datasets.

The system is intended to assist or automate discretionary reasoning—not imitate internet definitions.

---

# 3. Core Principles

## Principle 1 — Evidence First

No hypothesis is accepted because it is commonly taught.

Every hypothesis must survive empirical testing.

Examples:

- Rejection Block
- Fair Value Gap
- Order Block
- SMT
- Liquidity Sweep
- OTE
- Judas Swing
- Session Manipulation

All are research hypotheses.

None are assumptions.

---

## Principle 2 — Context Before Entry

Entries are never researched in isolation.

Market context must always be determined before evaluating any entry model.

The research hierarchy is:

Market Context

↓

Liquidity Context

↓

Session Context

↓

Microstructure Context

↓

Entry Validation

↓

Execution

An entry that performs well without context is preferred.

An entry requiring context must demonstrate incremental value.

---

## Principle 3 — Layer Independence

Each research layer must be measurable independently.

The contribution of every layer must be quantified.

No layer may hide weaknesses behind another.

Example:

If HTF bias contributes nothing after liquidity filters are applied, this must be documented.

---

## Principle 4 — Scientific Neutrality

Research questions must be phrased as:

"Does X improve Y?"

Never:

"Implement X."

The research engine exists to reject false hypotheses as efficiently as possible.

---

# 4. Research Philosophy

The project follows a hypothesis-driven workflow.

Every research task must contain:

- hypothesis
- measurable definition
- required data
- success criteria
- failure criteria
- limitations
- validation method

Research without measurable definitions is not permitted.

---

# 5. Project Scope

The current scope is limited to:

Market:

XAUUSD Spot

Primary execution horizon:

Intraday

Primary research horizon:

New York Session

Primary macro focus:

United States scheduled economic events

Future markets may be added later.

Research conclusions from one instrument must never automatically transfer to another.

---

# 6. Completed Foundation

The following stages are considered complete.

✓ Historical market dataset

✓ Tick normalization

✓ Bid/Ask normalization

✓ Broker timezone validation

✓ Economic calendar

✓ Stage 1 timing research

Current evidence:

- 08:30 represents a meaningful market regime change.
- 09:30 represents a general repricing window.
- No independent 10:00 directional edge has been established for XAUUSD.
- News acts as market context rather than directional prediction.

These conclusions remain subject to future validation.

---

---

# 8. Research Workflow

Every new hypothesis follows the same lifecycle.

1. Define the hypothesis.

2. Create objective definitions.

3. Build isolated research tools.

4. Validate data quality.

5. Run historical analysis.

6. Perform robustness checks.

7. Perform sensitivity analysis.

8. Produce reproducible reports.

9. Decide:

- Accepted
- Rejected
- Inconclusive

No production code is modified during research.

---

# 9. Statistical Standards

Every research project must report:

- sample size
- bootstrap confidence intervals
- robustness checks
- sensitivity analysis
- data exclusions
- known limitations

When sample sizes are insufficient:

the result must be labeled:

"Inconclusive."

Not:

"False."

---

# 10. Data Integrity Rules

Historical data may never be modified to improve results.

Missing data must be documented.

Ambiguous data must remain ambiguous.

No synthetic labels may be introduced without documentation.

No future information may influence historical decisions.

---

# 11. Production Separation

Research code and production code remain isolated.

Research may:

- create tools
- create datasets
- create reports
- create validation scripts

Research may never:

- modify production strategy logic,
- alter execution behavior,
- change live defaults,
- silently replace datasets.

Promotion to production requires explicit approval.

---

# 12. Definition Policy

Every market concept used in this project must eventually receive:

- an objective definition,
- measurable criteria,
- failure conditions,
- validation rules.

Until then:

all concepts remain research objects rather than established truths.

---

# 13. Decision Policy

The system prefers rejecting weak hypotheses over keeping attractive but unsupported ideas.

A discarded hypothesis is considered successful research if sufficient evidence exists.

Negative findings are valuable.

The Stage 1 rejection of an independent XAUUSD 10:00 timing edge is an example of successful research.

---

# 14. Success Criteria

The project is considered successful if it produces a trading framework whose decisions can be explained as a sequence of validated research findings rather than discretionary intuition.

Every execution decision should ultimately be traceable to objective evidence produced by this research program.

---

## Part 2 — Market Context Framework

**Version:** 0.1 Draft

This document defines the research architecture for objective market context evaluation.

It intentionally avoids assuming the correctness of any discretionary trading methodology.

Every context layer defined below represents a research domain whose contribution must be measured independently before integration.

---

# 15. Market Context Philosophy

The market is assumed to exist in different structural regimes.

An execution model should not ask:

> "Is there an entry?"

Instead it should ask:

> "What market environment currently exists?"

Only after market context is established may entry models be evaluated.

The objective of this layer is to classify market state—not to generate trades.

---

# 16. Context Hierarchy

Every trading decision must be evaluated from the highest structural timeframe downward.

The research order is fixed.

Higher Timeframe Context

↓

Higher Timeframe Liquidity

↓

Session Context

↓

Intraday Liquidity Engineering

↓

Intermarket Confirmation

↓

Entry Validation

Lower layers must never override higher-layer conclusions without explicit evidence.

---

# 17. Higher Timeframe Context

## Objective

Determine whether directional market bias exists before New York execution begins.

The existence of HTF bias is currently a research hypothesis.

It has not yet been accepted.

---

Candidate inputs include (but are not limited to):

- Weekly structure
- Daily structure
- 4H structure
- Premium / Discount positioning
- Market delivery state
- Swing sequencing
- Structural displacement
- Liquidity objectives

None of these inputs are considered validated.

Each must be researched separately.

---

Candidate outputs:

Bullish Bias

Bearish Bias

Neutral

Indeterminate

Research must determine whether these states are statistically distinguishable.

---

# 18. Higher Timeframe Liquidity

HTF liquidity is treated as destination information rather than entry information.

Candidate liquidity pools include:

- Previous Day High
- Previous Day Low
- Previous Week High
- Previous Week Low
- Monday High
- Monday Low
- Monthly extremes
- Quarterly extremes
- External structural highs
- External structural lows

Candidate delivery arrays include:

- Fair Value Gap
- Order Block
- Breaker
- Inversion FVG
- Balanced Price Range
- Volume Imbalance

No array is assumed valid.

Each array requires objective research.

---

Research questions include:

Does interaction with HTF liquidity improve trade quality?

Does proximity matter?

Does sequence matter?

Does liquidity consumption change subsequent probabilities?

---

# 19. Structural Object Validation

Objects commonly referenced by discretionary traders must not be treated as binary.

Example:

Order Block

Research shall determine:

- objective identification
- structural origin
- displacement quality
- liquidity relationship
- mitigation behavior
- failure behavior

The project should ultimately produce a quality score rather than a simple True/False label.

The same methodology applies to:

- FVG
- Breaker
- IFVG
- Rejection Block
- Mitigation Block
- Balanced Price Range

---

# 20. Session Context

Trading sessions represent structural environments rather than trade signals.

Research currently focuses on:

Asia

London

New York

Candidate research topics:

- Session range
- Session volatility
- Session overlap
- Relative position
- Session expansion
- Session compression
- Session imbalance

The contribution of each session must be evaluated independently.

---

# 21. Session Liquidity

Candidate liquidity objects include:

Asia High

Asia Low

London High

London Low

Opening ranges

Overnight extremes

Internal session highs/lows

Research questions:

Does prior session liquidity predict New York behavior?

Does liquidity removal increase directional probability?

Does untouched liquidity carry predictive value?

No assumption is made.

---

# 22. Intraday Liquidity Engineering

This layer investigates whether liquidity is intentionally engineered before major trading windows.

Candidate observations include:

- pre-08:30 displacement
- pre-session sweep
- engineered equal highs/lows
- compression before expansion
- stop-run behavior
- false breakout behavior

Research objective:

Determine whether measurable liquidity engineering exists.

If detected:

Measure frequency, persistence and predictive value.

---

# 23. News Context

Macroeconomic events define market regime.

They do not define trade direction.

Current evidence indicates:

08:30

Meaningful regime change.

09:30

General repricing window.

10:00

No independent directional edge established.

Future evidence may revise these conclusions.

News should therefore enter the framework as context.

Not execution.

Research questions include:

Does news alter volatility?

Does news alter execution quality?

Does news alter HTF interaction?

Does news strengthen or weaken structural setups?

---

# 24. Session Interaction

Research shall investigate interactions between:

Asia

↓

London

↓

New York

Questions include:

Does Asia expansion reduce London probability?

Does London sweep influence New York continuation?

Does New York seek remaining session liquidity?

No relationships are assumed.

---

# 25. Intermarket Context (SMT)

SMT is considered a research hypothesis.

Not all observed divergences are meaningful.

Candidate validation variables include:

- timing alignment
- session alignment
- structural alignment
- liquidity alignment
- displacement quality
- persistence
- resolution

The project must distinguish between:

Random divergence

↓

Meaningful divergence

↓

Actionable divergence

Research should produce objective quality metrics.

---

# 26. Context Scoring

The final system may combine validated context layers into a probabilistic context score.

This score is not intended to produce entries.

It represents environmental quality.

Illustrative example only:

HTF Context

Liquidity Context

Session Context

News Context

SMT Context

↓

Context Score

The weighting of each component must be learned from research rather than assigned manually.

---

# 27. Independence Requirement

Every context layer must first demonstrate standalone value.

Only then may interactions between layers be researched.

Example:

HTF Bias

↓

validated independently

↓

HTF Bias + Liquidity

↓

validated

↓

HTF Bias + Liquidity + SMT

↓

validated

Interaction effects must never be assumed.

---

# 28. Exit Criteria

A context hypothesis is considered accepted only if it demonstrates:

- reproducibility
- statistical robustness
- stability across periods
- executable usefulness

Otherwise it must be classified as:

Rejected

or

Inconclusive

No hypothesis remains permanently accepted.

Future evidence may overturn previous conclusions.

## Part 3 — Entry Validation, Execution & Statistical Validation

**Version:** 0.1 Draft

---

# 29. Entry Philosophy

The objective of the execution engine is not to predict the market.

The objective is to participate only when validated market conditions are present.

Execution is therefore the final layer of the research hierarchy.

No execution decision may ignore higher context layers.

---

# 30. Entry Hierarchy

The execution pipeline shall remain fixed.

Higher Timeframe Context

↓

Liquidity Context

↓

Session Context

↓

News Context

↓

Intraday Structure

↓

Entry Validation

↓

Risk Validation

↓

Execution

No lower layer may compensate for an invalid higher layer.

---

# 31. Entry Validation Framework

The project does not recognize predefined entry models as objectively valid.

Each entry concept is treated as an independent research object.

Candidate entry families include:

- Rejection Block
- Fair Value Gap Re-entry
- Order Block Reaction
- Breaker Continuation
- Mitigation Block
- OTE
- Liquidity Sweep Reversal
- Displacement Continuation

Each candidate must independently demonstrate:

- repeatability
- objective detectability
- executable edge
- robustness

---

# 32. Confirmation Philosophy

Entries should not be evaluated as isolated candle patterns.

Research must determine whether an entry derives its value from:

- higher timeframe context
- liquidity interaction
- session timing
- structural confirmation
- execution quality

The incremental contribution of each confirmation layer must be measured.

---

# 33. Objective Definitions

Every execution object must ultimately receive:

Objective identification rules.

Required context.

Failure conditions.

Invalidation rules.

Expiration rules.

Known limitations.

Until objective definitions exist, discretionary terminology must remain descriptive only.

---

# 34. Target Selection

Take-profit methodology represents a research problem.

It must not be hard-coded.

Candidate destination models include:

- HTF liquidity
- LTF liquidity
- opposing session liquidity
- structural imbalance repair
- measured expansion
- volatility-adjusted objectives

Research shall determine:

- expected reach probability
- time-to-target
- adverse excursion
- favorable excursion
- interaction with market regime

---

# 35. Stop Placement

Stop-loss methodology must be evaluated independently from entry methodology.

Candidate approaches include:

- structural invalidation
- liquidity invalidation
- volatility-adjusted stops
- displacement failure
- fixed risk
- adaptive risk

No stop model is assumed superior.

---

# 36. Execution Quality

Research must distinguish between:

Theoretical opportunity

↓

Executable opportunity

Execution analysis should consider:

- spread
- bid/ask movement
- liquidity conditions
- execution latency (where measurable)
- available market resolution

Theoretical performance is insufficient.

---

# 37. Risk Framework

Risk management is considered an independent research layer.

Research topics include:

- fixed fractional risk
- adaptive volatility risk
- context-adjusted risk
- news-adjusted risk
- drawdown control
- correlation control

Risk must never be optimized using future information.

---

# 38. Position Management

Position management shall be evaluated separately from entries.

Candidate research topics include:

- partial exits
- scale-in
- scale-out
- trailing logic
- break-even behavior
- liquidity-based exit
- time-based exit

Entry quality and management quality must never be conflated.

---

# 39. Performance Measurement

Every experiment must report:

- trade count
- win rate
- expectancy
- average R
- median R
- MAE
- MFE
- holding time
- drawdown
- volatility exposure
- execution costs

No single metric is sufficient.

---

# 40. Statistical Validation

Every accepted finding must survive:

- bootstrap validation
- sensitivity analysis
- parameter variation
- outlier analysis
- spread filtering
- yearly comparison

Failure under robustness testing requires reclassification.

---

# 41. Holdout Validation

Historical optimization alone is insufficient.

Every promising hypothesis must be evaluated on unseen data.

Accepted workflow:

Research

↓

Development

↓

Holdout

↓

Walk Forward

↓

Paper Trading

↓

Production Candidate

---

# 42. Walk Forward Policy

Walk-forward testing must preserve chronological order.

No future observations may influence earlier model decisions.

Rolling evaluation is preferred over a single historical optimization.

---

# 43. Production Gate

Promotion from research to production requires:

- objective definition
- reproducible implementation
- robustness validation
- holdout success
- walk-forward success
- paper trading success
- code review
- explicit approval

Research success alone is insufficient.

---

# 44. Failure Policy

Rejected hypotheses remain documented.

Negative findings are preserved.

Previously rejected ideas may be revisited only when:

- new data,
- new methodology,
- or new evidence

becomes available.

---

# 45. Research Documentation

Every completed study must produce:

- Markdown summary
- Machine-readable JSON
- CSV datasets
- Validation report
- Quality report
- Metadata
- Reproducible command history

No undocumented experiment is considered valid.

---

# 46. Model Governance

The project favors simplicity.

When two models produce statistically indistinguishable results:

The simpler model is preferred.

Complexity must justify itself through measurable improvement.

---

# 47. Long-Term Objective

The completed system should function as a Context Engine rather than a signal generator.

Its purpose is to continuously evaluate:

- market regime,
- structural quality,
- execution quality,
- risk quality,

before permitting any trade.

Execution should become the consequence of validated context—not the starting point.

---

**End of Part 3**
