## Documentation Policy

- Treat these documents as research and implementation notes, not trading advice.
- Do not present planned work as implemented behavior.
- Keep assumptions and limitations visible.
- Keep raw third-party source material out of the public repository unless redistribution rights are clear.

## Local-Only Material

`docs/raw_sources/` is reserved for local research-source files and is ignored by git. Do not commit third-party PDFs or other source files unless the project has clear rights to redistribute them.
