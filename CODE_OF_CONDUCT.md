# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

AurumFlow is a research-focused open-source project. Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good intent, and keep technical discussions grounded in evidence.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be direct about technical risks without personal criticism.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Credit prior work where appropriate.
- Avoid exaggerated claims about trading outcomes.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publicly sharing private information without permission.
- Deliberately misleading users about risk or performance.
- Sustained off-topic disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this code. Maintainers may also limit participation for contributors who repeatedly disregard project norms.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository owner's preferred contact channel. If private contact is not available, open a minimal public issue asking for a private maintainer contact without including sensitive details.
