# Hypothesis Register

This register freezes the first-pass questions before any real XAUUSD event-study output is examined. Source IDs refer to `source_register.csv`. Labels are deliberately conservative: **Literature-supported**, **Practitioner-derived**, **Inferred**, **Exploratory**, or **Rejected before testing**.

The null for a directional or expectancy hypothesis is zero incremental effect after costs. The null for comparisons is no difference between the predeclared cohorts. Confidence intervals use session-level resampling; p-values, when reported, are descriptive and adjusted within each hypothesis family. A statistically significant result is not sufficient unless its sign is stable across years, directions and event categories and its effective sample is adequate.

## Multiplicity and stability gates

The hypotheses form five families: event-time movement (H01–H06), path/lifecycle behavior (H07–H10), 10:00 effects (H11–H12/H18), entry geometry/costs (H13–H16), and model stability/conditioning (H17/H19/H20). False-discovery-rate adjusted p-values may accompany effect sizes, but the primary evidence is a bootstrap confidence interval and held-out effect size.

No strategy family advances to future production research unless all of the following hold:

1. non-negative after-cost expectancy in the final chronological holdout, with a 95% bootstrap interval that is not materially below zero;
2. adequate sample size and effective independent event count, reported rather than hidden by trade count;
3. positive or near-zero expectancy in most years and both directions, with no single event category producing the result;
4. survival under the stressed spread/slippage scenario and modest parameter perturbations;
5. no use of lifecycle outcomes or future-confirmed structures as entry information.

These gates can reject a model. They cannot establish production readiness by themselves.
