# Deterministic Concept Definitions

This file converts practitioner language into predeclared research variables. Source IDs refer to `source_register.csv`. None of the definitions asserts institutional intent.

## Common conventions

- Input resolution: primary 1-minute OHLC; 3-minute bars are aggregated from closed 1-minute bars with left-closed, left-labeled intervals.
- Time zone: timestamps are stored timezone-aware and converted with IANA `America/New_York`. Fixed UTC offsets are prohibited.
- Intervals are half-open: `[start, end)`. For example, the 08:30–08:35 impulse uses bars opening at 08:30 through 08:34.
- Primary pre-news range: 07:30–08:30. Secondary range: 08:00–08:30.
- London primary definition: 08:00–12:00 `Europe/London`, projected onto the New York trading date. Sensitivity definition: 02:00–05:00 `America/New_York`. The two definitions are never pooled silently.
- Previous-day levels: prior complete New York calendar trading day, excluding the current day. A sensitivity view uses prior COMEX trade date.
- `tick_buffer`: `max(2 * observed_min_price_increment, 0.05 * rolling_20d_median_1m_true_range)`. Sensitivity: 1, 2 and 3 ticks and 0%, 5%, 10% of median true range.
- Rolling baselines use only prior sessions. Same-time range median uses the same clock bucket over the prior 60 eligible sessions, minimum 20. ADR uses the prior 20 complete New York days, minimum 10.
- A *confirmed swing high* of width `k` is bar `i` whose high exceeds the highs of the `k` bars on both sides; it becomes observable only at the close of bar `i+k`. A swing low is symmetric. Primary `k=2`; sensitivity `k=1,3,5`.
- Direction `d` is `+1` for bullish and `-1` for bearish. Directional distance from price `a` to `b` is `d * (b-a)`.
- Any rule requiring a future close is timestamped when that close becomes available. Orders cannot fill before the next bar unless the strategy explicitly uses a market order at that confirmation close and a separate adverse-slippage assumption.

## Formalized concepts

| Liquidity sweep | Breach an obvious high/low and rapidly return through it ([S32](https://algobars.com/concepts/liquidity-sweep/)); practitioner sources infer clustered stops. | For a predeclared level `L`, bullish-side sweep: `high >= L + buffer` and a close `<= L` occurs within `n` closed bars; bearish-side symmetric. | Rejection block | Significant wick rejection at a structural level; zone is the wick or body-to-wick interval ([S28](https://ictflow.com/blog/ict-rejection-block-explained)). | Only after a predeclared sweep: select the first close-back candle. | OTE | 62%–79% retracement of an impulse, with 70.5% often highlighted ([S27](https://www.quantum-algo.com/glossary/optimal-trade-entry/)). | Anchor exclusively to the predeclared 08:30 impulse low-to-high for bullish or high-to-low for bearish. ## Lifecycle classification

Lifecycle states are assigned after the 10:30 bar closes. They are descriptive outcomes and must not be used to select entries. To make states mutually exclusive, apply this precedence:

1. **Double-sided liquidity sweep:** both primary pre-news boundaries are breached by the buffer between 08:30 and 10:30, or both 08:30 impulse extremes are breached after 09:30.
2. **08:30 false breakout and full reversal:** 08:30 breaks one primary pre-news boundary, re-enters before acceptance, and reaches the opposite primary boundary by 10:30.
3. **08:30 impulse followed by 09:30 liquidity sweep and reversal:** after 09:30, price breaches the 08:30 impulse extreme in its direction, closes back through it, confirms opposite MSS, and reaches the impulse midpoint; it must not meet state 1 or 2.
4. **Exhausted move:** meets the exhaustion rule above and not states 1–3.
5. **08:30 impulse followed by partial retracement and continuation:** retracement is 25%–75%, then price makes a new impulse-direction extreme and closes on that side of the impulse midpoint by 10:30.
6. **08:30 continuation:** extension is at least 50% of impulse, retracement is less than 25%, and price closes on the directional side of midpoint.
7. **Range/chop:** every remaining day, with subflags for small impulse, no acceptance and total normalized range.

Precedence and thresholds are sensitivity-tested, but the primary specification is frozen before real-data evaluation.

## Candidate entry geometry

All entries require a strategy-family trigger; geometry alone is not a setup.

| Geometry | Causal activation | Price | Fill rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Market after confirmed MSS | MSS bar closes | Next-bar open plus adverse slippage; separate idealized MSS-close view | Always filled if next bar exists and spread is valid |
| First FVG proximal | First post-trigger FVG closes | Edge nearest current price | Pending from next bar; expire after predeclared minutes or at 10:00/10:30 event boundary |
| First FVG midpoint | Same | Arithmetic zone midpoint | Same |
| First FVG distal | Same | Farther edge | Same |
| Rejection block | Deterministic rejection candle closes | Primary midpoint; edges as sensitivity | Same; exploratory only |
| 50% impulse | Full impulse window closes | 0.50 retracement | Pending; no retroactive fill inside the impulse window |
| 62% impulse | Same | 0.62 retracement | Same |
| 75% impulse | Same | 0.75 retracement | Same |
| OTE zone | Same | First touch of 0.62–0.79; 0.705 single-level sensitivity | Same |

Stops are placed beyond the causal sweep/impulse invalidation extreme plus the cost buffer. Targets are 1R, 1.5R, 2R and predeclared opposing levels. If stop and target are both touched in one OHLC bar, the conservative primary result assumes stop first. Tick/bid-ask data are required to relax that assumption.

## Excluded from the first test

- deliberate institutional manipulation;
- inferred hidden order blocks with no deterministic candle selector;
- narrative daily bias chosen after observing the New York session;
- “clean,” “strong,” or “obvious” structures without numerical thresholds;
- a 10:00 key-open effect that does not separate scheduled 10:00 releases;
- any lifecycle label used before its confirmation time.
